# Activity Intelligence evaluation scenarios

Synthetic-only checks for Weekly Intelligence input and reasoning constraints:

1. Research experiment, benchmark and paper draft: direct research progress and outputs; no drift.
2. Release-heavy engineering week with no research evidence: valuable output, but possible research opportunity cost.
3. Reading/tutorial week: learning is not automatically an output.
4. OS/RISC-V exploration: exploratory/supporting, not waste.
5. Maintenance-heavy bugfix/release work: maintenance can have value and output.
6. Long ChatGPT discussion without a deliverable: activity is not output.
7. ChatGPT discussion producing an architecture decision or implementation: output and progress.
8. Deep systems learning while paper delivery is primary: high progress may be supporting alignment.
9. One rest day amid six productive days: no drift signal.
10. Five or more active days away from highest priority goal: evidence may support opportunity cost.
11. Goal priority change: canonical goal projection and weekly hash change.
12. One or two recorded days: sparse-data output remains cautious; no strong drift conclusion.

All scenarios assert structure, evidence allow-lists, input boundaries, cache behavior and classifications—not exact model prose.
